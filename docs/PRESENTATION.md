# A five-minute client walkthrough

1. Start at `/`. Show the editorial title entrance, original court photography, direct reservation bar, interactive private-court/open-play selector, club story, and manually controlled courtside gallery. Choose a day and play type in the reservation bar to open the booking calendar. Mention that branding, contact details, concept images and final venue pricing will be customized.
2. Open `/demo`. The lavender banner identifies presentation mode. Overview connects sample court reservations, payments, retail sales and low-stock warnings.
3. Open **Point of sale**. Add Spring Water and Sports Drink. Charge ₱95. Enter ₱100 received, show ₱5 change, check the received-payment confirmation, and complete the sale. Show the sample transaction slip. No money or hosted data is touched.
4. Open **Reservations**. Verify Jamie's submitted GCash reference after checking the demo verification box. Alex's booking already has a ₱200 reservation fee; **Collect balance** requests only the remaining ₱200. This illustrates booking status independently from payment status.
5. Open **Inventory**. Water and Sports Drink stocks reflect the sale. Demonstrate an adjustment with a reason, or add a sample product and record stock received.
6. Open **Transactions**. Reopen a receipt. Demonstrate a manager's full refund and returned-stock option using sample data.
7. Open **Reports**. Explain cash versus GCash, collections versus booking value, and refunds. Export a CSV or close the sample shift with an actual cash count to show variance.

Use **Reset sample data** to restore the presentation. Reloading resets the in-memory demonstration too.

Position this as a first working presentation. Final client branding, real Supabase setup/owner access, GCash recipient details, printer validation, operating policies and production acceptance testing precede live use.
